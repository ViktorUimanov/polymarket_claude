### Why this edge exists

The market is pricing regime collapse at 29.5% — nearly 4× my fair value estimate. The gap is explained by the market over-weighting the novelty of the Mojtaba succession and the scale of protests, while under-weighting:

1. **IRGC institutional loyalty**: The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps is the actual power in Iran. It has not shown any sign of internal fracture. Without IRGC defections, regime collapse is structurally impossible regardless of street protests.

2. **Succession completed without visible split**: Mojtaba elevated quickly, no reporting of rival claimants or IRGC generals refusing allegiance. The transition was smooth by Iranian standards.

3. **Historical base rate**: Iran survived the 1980–88 Iran-Iraq war (far more costly), 2009/2019/2022 mass protests, targeted assassinations of IRGC leaders. The regime is highly resilient.

4. **Resolution criteria require full collapse**: Not just leadership change, not just protests — the Supreme Leader office, Guardian Council, AND IRGC control must all cease to function. This is an extremely high bar.

5. **Wartime consolidation effect**: Authoritarian regimes typically use external military threats to suppress internal dissent more effectively, not less.

### What I'm getting wrong if NO loses

The only realistic path to YES by June 30: a rapid cascade of IRGC senior officers defecting to opposition + US/Israel ground operations that physically destroy IRGC command structure. Both would need to happen within 3.5 months. Current reporting shows no sign of either.
