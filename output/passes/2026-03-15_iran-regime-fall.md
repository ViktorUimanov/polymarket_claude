**Date:** 2026-03-15

**Market Price:** YES 2.5%, NO 97.5%
**Volume:** $33,723,117

## Why I Passed

While the Iran war (US/Israel vs Iran, started ~Feb 28) is the dominant geopolitical event, "regime fall" by March 31 is a very specific and unlikely outcome. Reasons:

1. **Regime resilience**: The Islamic Republic has survived decades of sanctions, internal protests (2009, 2019, 2022), and international pressure.
2. **Short time horizon**: 16 days is extremely short for a regime change of this magnitude.
3. **Military not defeated**: The US/Israel struck Iranian nuclear facilities and military assets, but the IRGC and government structure remain intact.
4. **No organized opposition**: Unlike Syria in 2024, there is no organized armed opposition on the verge of taking power.

Market at 2.5% is likely CORRECT or even slightly overpriced. I would estimate ~1.5-2% fair value, so there's a tiny edge on NO. But this is a political tail-risk bet that's hard to price confidently, and 97.5% NO at $0.975 only offers 2.5 cents per dollar with meaningful black-swan risk (US ground troops entering Iran, etc.).

**Decision:** Pass. The edge is too small relative to the uncertainty and complexity.
