## Why I Passed

The market is essentially resolved at 99.7%. The Fed has clearly telegraphed a pause at this meeting, and the market consensus is overwhelming. There is zero edge in this market — buying YES at $0.997 gives less than 1 cent upside per dollar while carrying some residual "surprise cut/hike" risk.

This market resolves March 18 in 3 days. Both the YES and the arbitrarily low NO price make this untradeable.

## Lesson
- When a market is at 99%+, don't chase it. The edge is gone.
- These markets are useful for market structure understanding (e.g., the Fed meeting is coming up) but not for profit.
