## Why Considered

Active US-Iran war, Trump described as a dealmaker. Related Polymarket ceasefire market (before China visit) showed 25%. Initial estimate suggested ~8.5pp edge.

## Why Revised Down

**Iran FM explicit denial** (March 16): Araghchi stated "we do not ask for ceasefire, but this war must end." Iran FM is actively signaling they are NOT seeking a ceasefire.

**Both sides still striking**: As of March 15, US and Iran continue air strikes with no letup.

**Trump on deal**: "Not ready to make a deal because terms aren't good enough yet" — not negotiating in good faith.

**14 days left**: Very short timeframe for formal mutual agreement requiring "clear public confirmation from BOTH governments."

## Why I'm Passing

After incorporating Iran FM's explicit statement and the ongoing strikes, my revised fair value (14-16%) converges with the market price (13.5%). Edge is at most 2pp — well below 4pp minimum.

## Lesson

Initial estimates on conflict/ceasefire markets should explicitly incorporate statements from both parties, not just Trump's dealmaking reputation. The FM's explicit denial should have been the first data point checked.
