# Learnings: Oscars / Awards Prediction Markets

**Date:** 2026-03-15
**Source:** 98th Academy Awards — Major upsets across all categories

---

## Key Lessons

### 1. Always Check ALL Market Options Before Deciding (CRITICAL)
**What happened:** I focused my analysis on "Sinners vs One Battle After Another" and ignored Bugonia entirely. Bugonia won Best Picture. I could have bought Bugonia YES at perhaps 2-5% and made a massive profit.

**Rule:** Before evaluating which of the top candidates to bet on, scan ALL active market options. The winner may not be who you expect.

### 2. The Academy Has Extreme Tail Risk
**What happened:** In 2026, EVERY major category went against consensus. PTA (92.5% Director), MBJ (61.5% Actor), Amy Madigan (55.5% Supporting Actress), Sean Penn (77.5% Supporting Actor) — all lost.

**Rule:** Never assume 90%+ probability in Oscars markets. The Academy is more contrarian than guild bodies. Distribute at least 10-15% of probability to long shots in every Oscars market.

### 3. SAG Ensemble Win ≠ Best Picture (Revised)
**What happened:** I argued for Sinners based on the SAG Ensemble win (historically strongest BP predictor). Sinners won SAG but lost BP to Bugonia — a film that won neither SAG nor DGA.

**Updated view:** SAG is still a useful signal but NOT predictive enough to overcome 4+ other guild losses AND to ignore dark horses. The SAG win for Sinners should have raised Sinners from 19.9% → maybe 25-28%, not 28-32%.

### 4. DGA + PGA Double Win Is Also Not Infallible (2026 Update)
**What happened:** "One Battle After Another" won DGA + PGA + BAFTA + Golden Globe + Critics Choice but did NOT win Best Picture or Best Director at the Oscars.

**Historical rate update:** While DGA+PGA wins historically predict BP ~85%+, this year broke that pattern completely. The confidence interval should be wider than historical data suggests.

### 5. Lanthimos/Auteur Dark Horses Are Dangerous to Ignore
**What happened:** Bugonia was made by Yorgos Lanthimos (The Favourite, Poor Things, Kinds of Kindness) with Emma Stone. Lanthimos has won Director before (Chloe Zhao won for HAMNET, not Bugonia — but Emma Stone won Actress). The Academy has a track record of rewarding auteur filmmakers.

**Rule:** When a known acclaimed director (Lanthimos, Malick, Anderson, etc.) has a film nominated, always check their market price regardless of their guild performance. Auteur filmmakers can surprise.

### 6. Oscars Markets Have Very High Variance Even For "Sure Things"
**Trade rule going forward:** Max 1-2% bankroll on any individual Oscars category market.

## What I Should Have Done

1. Surveyed ALL Best Picture nominees and their market prices
2. Given Bugonia (Yorgos Lanthimos) at minimum 5-8% probability
3. Sized position at 1% not 2% given the known uncertainty in Oscars
4. Possibly passed entirely — no clear edge when the field is this unusual
