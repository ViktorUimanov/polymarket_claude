# General Trading Principles (Developed from Experience)

**Started:** 2026-03-15

---

## Principles

### 1. Check the FULL market slate before betting (CRITICAL — learned from Oscars)
Don't anchor on the top 2-3 candidates and miss the actual winner. The dark horse that you ignored might win. Before placing any bet, catalog ALL active outcomes in the market.

**How to apply:** When exploring a market event, always look at the full list of outcomes and their prices, even the 1-5% ones.

### 2. Markets near 99%+ have no edge — pass
When a market is priced at 97-99.9% YES, the upside is tiny and the downside risk is asymmetric. There might be a tiny short/NO opportunity but rarely worth the effort.

**Example:** Fed rate decision at 99.7%, measles 1450 at 97.7%. Pass on both.

### 3. Settlement price ≠ intraday price (CRITICAL for commodity markets)
Polymarket's commodity markets use OFFICIAL SETTLEMENT prices, not intraday highs/lows. News headlines about "oil nearly hit $120" refer to intraday trades. Settlement prices are typically significantly different.

**Example:** WTI hit $119.94 intraday on March 9, but the SETTLEMENT that day was $94.65 (-21%).

### 4. Distinguish WTI from Brent crude
News headlines often say "oil at $100+" but mean Brent. Polymarket's CL markets use WTI (NYMEX). WTI typically trades $4-5 below Brent.

**Example:** Brent closed above $100 on March 12-13; WTI was at $98.71 (below $100).

### 5. Understand the resolution oracle for every market
Before betting, read the full resolution description. "How does this market resolve?" is the most important question. Different oracles have different characteristics:
- CDC case counter: lags real-time data
- CME settlement price: end-of-day, different from intraday
- Chatbot Arena: specific time (March 31, 12 PM ET)

### 6. Size positions based on confidence and variance
- High confidence, short-dated: 3-4% bankroll
- High confidence, long-dated: 2-3% bankroll
- Medium confidence, any: 1-2% bankroll
- Low confidence/informational bet: 0.5-1% bankroll

**Never exceed 5% on any single market.**

### 7. Don't chase markets with tiny edges
If a market is fairly priced and you see only 1-2% edge, the transaction costs and market uncertainty typically wipe it out. Only bet when you see 4%+ clear edge.

### 8. High variance events need wider probability distributions
Oscars, elections, geopolitical events — these have fat tails. Don't assign 92%+ to any Oscars category winner, even if guild consensus is overwhelming.

### 9. Post-mortem every loss immediately
Document what happened, what I missed, and what I should do differently. Never make the same mistake twice.

### 10. Research before every trade — no exceptions
The single rule I must never break: Research before placing any trade. The research is the work; the trade is just recording the conclusion.
