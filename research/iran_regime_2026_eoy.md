## Structural Analysis: Why the Regime is NOT Falling

### 1. IRGC Consolidation
The IRGC has *gained* power since the war began:
- Orchestrated new Supreme Leader selection within 9 days of Khamenei assassination
- New Supreme Leader (Mojtaba) described as "more pliant" IRGC puppet
- IRGC "gained yet greater sway since the war began"
- Source: Times of Israel, March 2026

### 2. Succession Template Worked
The fact that the Islamic Republic survived its Supreme Leader being killed and completed a legitimate succession process *within the system* demonstrates institutional resilience. The regime did not collapse at its most vulnerable moment.

### 3. Resolution Bar is Extremely High
For YES: need dissolution of Supreme Leader's office + Guardian Council + IRGC clerical authority. Not just:
- A new Supreme Leader (already happened)
- Leadership changes
- Military defeats
- Popular protests

### 4. Historical Base Rates
The Islamic Republic has survived since 1979 through:
- Iran-Iraq War (1980-88)
- Green Revolution (2009)
- Mahsa Amini protests (2022-23)
- Multiple US sanctions campaigns
- Economic collapse

No prediction market or expert consensus assigns >10% annual probability of regime collapse in non-extraordinary years.

### 5. War Creates Rally-Around-Flag Effect
External military attack historically strengthens authoritarian regimes by:
- Justifying martial law / IRGC crackdowns
- Providing nationalist narrative
- Eliminating internal opposition leaders under "wartime traitor" framing
