## Background

In late February 2026, the US and Israel struck Iran. Iran retaliated by closing the Strait of Hormuz, through which ~20% of global oil supply passes. This triggered the largest single-week surge in crude oil futures history (+35% for WTI in the week of March 2-6).

## WTI vs Brent

**Critical distinction:** Polymarket's CL markets use **WTI (NYMEX)**, not Brent.

- Brent crude settled above $100 on March 12 and 13 (Bloomberg confirmed)
- WTI settled at $98.71 on March 13 — just below $100
- WTI-Brent spread: approximately -$4 to -$5 (WTI is cheaper)

**Source of confusion:** News headlines saying "Oil closes above $100" often refer to Brent. WTI has NOT yet settled above $100 as of March 13, 2026.

---

## Settlement vs Intraday — THE KEY INSIGHT

CME settlement price ≠ intraday high.

**March 9 data (most extreme day):**
- WTI intraday high: ~$119.94 (the famous "nearly hit $120" moment)
- WTI official CME settlement: **$94.65** (21.1% BELOW intraday high)

This is because:
1. Intraday spikes are often driven by algorithmic/panic buying
2. Professional sellers (oil producers, hedgers) sell into spikes
3. Settlement is a volume-weighted average near close, not the extreme

**Implication:** For settlement-based Polymarket markets, the relevant price is significantly below what you read in "oil nearly hit $120" headlines.

## Scenarios

### Bull scenario (for HIGH markets):
- Hormuz remains blocked through end of March
- No diplomatic solution
- Supply shortfalls materialize in physical markets
- Settlement prices continue rising: $100 → $110 → $120 possible over 11 days
- **Probability:** ~40% that we see $120+ settlement

### Bear scenario (for LOW markets, bad for HIGH markets):
- Diplomatic breakthrough / Iran agrees to talks
- IEA emergency stock release (500M+ barrels discussed)
- Trump declares "mission accomplished" and pulls back
- Settlement crashes back toward $70-80
- **Probability:** ~25% that we see this before end of March

### Base scenario:
- Oil stays volatile between $85-$110 range
- $100 crossed on at least one day (very likely)
- $120 NOT reached at settlement
- **Probability:** ~35%
