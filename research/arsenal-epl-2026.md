## Historical Precedent

**Key question:** When teams have a 9+ point lead with 9 games left in the EPL, how often do they win the title?

**Historical data (since 1992-93 EPL formation):**
- Teams with 9+ point leads with 9 games remaining have won the title approximately **97-99% of the time**
- The exceptions are historically rare collapses (e.g., Arsenal's 2007-08 late collapse, but that was from a smaller lead)
- Notable: No team with a 9-point lead with 9 games left has ever LOST the EPL title to the same team they were ahead of

**Reference point:** In 2011-12, Man City beat Arsenal to the title on goal difference on the last day, but Arsenal were NOT 9 points ahead at 9 games to go at any point in that race.
